What "Managed" Actually Means

Security First

Every month we run a full security scan across your site, verify your SSL certificate is valid and correctly configured, check for unauthorised file changes, and confirm your firewall and spam filters are active. You're never one missed renewal away from a red-padlock warning killing your credibility.

Performance Monitoring

We monitor your site around the clock and receive instant alerts if your site goes down. On higher tiers, we run monthly speed audits, check your Core Web Vitals, and apply caching and image optimisation improvements — because a fast website isn't a luxury in South Africa's competitive market, it's a ranking factor.

Real Human Support

Support means WhatsApp and direct email to Gideon — not a ticket number, not a chatbot, not a 72-hour queue. We know your site, your business, and what "urgent" means for you. On our Galactic Growth plan, you get a dedicated support line and same-day response for anything that needs immediate attention.

Already Have a Website?

If we built your website, a base level of care is already built into your monthly retainer — hosting, backups, and monitoring are included. Our standalone care plans are for businesses whose websites were built by another developer, or for clients who want to upgrade to a higher tier with more content support, priority response, and advanced reporting.

Unsure what you're currently on? Drop us a WhatsApp and we'll go through it with you — no sales pitch, plain language.
