Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Celestial Visions ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number and WhatsApp number
  • Business name and website URL
  • Any information you voluntarily provide when contacting us

3. Why We Collect Your Information

We collect your information to:

  • Provide web design and digital marketing services
  • Send you quotes and proposals
  • Communicate with you about your project
  • Send service-related emails and follow-ups
  • Improve our services

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only for the purposes stated above. We will never sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

5. Email Communications

If you receive an email from us you did not request, you may opt out at any time by replying "unsubscribe" and we will remove you immediately from all future communications.

6. Data Storage

Your information is stored securely using industry-standard tools including Google Workspace and encrypted cloud storage. We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide our services.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information we hold
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforeg.org.za

8. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use Google Analytics to track visitor behaviour anonymously. No personally identifiable information is collected through cookies.
