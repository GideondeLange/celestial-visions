Facebook & Instagram Advertising

Put your business in front of thousands of potential customers in your area — with targeted Meta campaigns professionally managed for you from strategy to results.

Why Advertise on Meta?

Facebook and Instagram are the most widely used social platforms in South Africa, with millions of daily active users across every age group and demographic. Meta's advertising tools let you target people by location, age, interests, income level, and online behaviour — putting your business directly in front of your ideal customers at exactly the right moment, whether they're browsing their feed, watching Reels, or catching up on Stories.

What We Handle for You

We take on the full Meta advertising process so you can focus on running your business. From setting up your Meta Business Suite and installing the Facebook Pixel, to building your ad campaigns, crafting ad copy, targeting the right audiences, and analysing performance — we manage everything end-to-end and report back to you monthly with clear, straightforward results you can actually understand.

Built for South African Small Businesses

Unlike big-agency ad management that's priced for corporates, our advertising packages are designed specifically for South African small businesses and startups. Whether you're a hair salon in Boksburg, a construction company on the East Rand, or a boutique retailer in Johannesburg — we build campaigns around your specific goals, your local area, and your budget.

The key difference between our tiers isn't what we do — it's how much ad spend we're managing for you. A larger ad spend budget means more people see your ads, more often. Our management fee scales with that spend so you always get dedicated attention proportional to the size of your campaigns. We're transparent about what your money is doing and where the results are coming from.

How It Works

1. Strategy & Setup

We start by understanding your business, your target customers, and your goals. We then set up your Meta Business Suite, install the Facebook Pixel on your website, define your target audiences, and build the campaign structure before a single rand is spent on ads.

2. Campaign Launch

Once the strategy is in place, we create and launch your ad campaigns across Facebook and Instagram. We write the ad copy, advise on creative (images or video), set your audience targeting, and configure your budget to maximise reach and results from day one.

3. Optimise & Report

Advertising improves over time. We monitor your campaigns continuously, adjusting targeting, budgets, and creative based on real performance data. Every month you receive a clear report showing your reach, engagement, leads, and what your ad spend achieved.
