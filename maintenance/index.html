Website Maintenance

Your website isn't a once-off project — it needs regular care to stay secure, fast, and working for your business every single day. Our optional care plan takes it off your hands for R450 a month.

A Neglected Website is a Liability

Expired SSL certificates, broken contact forms, slow load times, and undetected malware don't just frustrate visitors — they actively damage your reputation and your Google ranking. In South Africa's competitive small-business landscape, a website that looks neglected tells potential customers that your business is the same. Regular maintenance keeps your site sharp, secure, and performing at its best every single month.

We Handle It So You Don't Have To

As a small business owner, your time is better spent running your business than worrying about security scans, caching configs, or whether your contact form still works. Our optional Care Plan is available with any website package — you get a dedicated point of contact who looks after hosting, updates, backups and small edits, without you needing to chase anyone.

How It Works

1. Onboarding

We access your website and hosting environment and check the current state of your site. If there are existing issues we identify them upfront and resolve them before the first billing cycle — no nasty surprises after you sign up.

2. Monthly Care

Every month we keep your hosting running, apply updates, and make sure your backups are in place. Small edits — updating business hours, adding a new service, swapping an image, tweaking copy — are applied promptly.

3. Direct Support

Need a change or have a question? WhatsApp or email us directly — you deal with the person who built your site, not a ticket queue.
