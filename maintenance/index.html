Website Maintenance

Your website isn't a once-off project — it needs regular care to stay secure, fast, and working for your business every single day. Here's exactly what we do every month.

A Neglected Website is a Liability

Expired SSL certificates, broken contact forms, slow load times, and undetected malware don't just frustrate visitors — they actively damage your reputation and your Google ranking. In South Africa's competitive small-business landscape, a website that looks neglected tells potential customers that your business is the same. Regular maintenance keeps your site sharp, secure, and performing at its best every single month.

We Handle It So You Don't Have To

As a small business owner, your time is better spent running your business than worrying about security scans, caching configs, or whether your contact form still works. Our maintenance work is built into every Website Care & Growth Plan — you get a dedicated point of contact who monitors, updates, and reports on your website every month, without you needing to chase anyone.

How It Works

1. Onboarding

We access your website and hosting environment, run a full audit of the current state, and set up our monitoring tools. If there are existing issues we identify them upfront and resolve them before the first billing cycle — no nasty surprises after you sign up.

2. Monthly Care

Every month we run all scheduled security scans, backup verifications, performance checks, and uptime reviews. On Nebula Business and Galactic Growth plans, content updates — updating business hours, adding a new service, swapping an image, tweaking copy — are applied promptly within your included hours.

3. Monthly Report

At the end of each month you receive a clear summary via WhatsApp or email: what was done, your site's uptime stats, performance metrics, and any recommendations for the month ahead. You're always in the loop without having to ask.
