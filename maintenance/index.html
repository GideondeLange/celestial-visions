Website Maintenance

Your website isn't a once-off project — it needs regular care to stay secure, fast, and working for your business every day.

A Neglected Website is a Liability

Outdated software, expired SSL certificates, broken links, and slow load times don't just frustrate visitors — they actively damage your reputation and your Google ranking. In South Africa's competitive small-business landscape, a website that looks neglected or feels slow tells potential customers that your business is the same. Regular maintenance keeps your site sharp, secure, and performing at its best every single month.

We Handle It So You Don't Have To

As a small business owner, your time is better spent on running your business than worrying about plugin updates, security scans, or whether your contact form still works. Our maintenance plans give you a dedicated point of contact who monitors, updates, and reports on your website every month — so you stay informed without having to get involved in the technical details.

What Maintenance Covers

Our maintenance service is a complete monthly care package for your website. We go beyond basic updates — we actively look for issues before they become problems, monitor your site's performance, and keep you updated with a clear monthly report so you always know your website is in good hands.

How It Works

1. Onboarding

We access your website and hosting environment, run a full audit of the current state, and set up our monitoring tools. If there are any existing issues we identify them upfront and resolve them as part of getting started — no nasty surprises later.

2. Monthly Care

Every month we run all scheduled updates, security scans, and performance checks. Content updates included in your plan are applied promptly — whether that's updating business hours, adding a new service, swapping an image, or tweaking copy.

3. Monthly Report

At the end of each month you receive a clear summary covering what was done, your site's uptime and performance stats, and any recommendations for the month ahead. You're always in the loop without having to chase anyone.
